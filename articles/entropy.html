The Universe Doesn't Care

25 July 2026 • 6 min read

The universe doesn't reward effort, nor does it punish failure. It simply follows the laws of physics.

Imagine a universe where nothing has purpose. Stars explode. Galaxies collide. Black holes swallow light.

Entropy Doesn't Hate You

Entropy isn't evil. It isn't trying to destroy order. It is simply the natural tendency for energy to spread.

"Nature has no favourites. It only has laws."

Once you understand this, the universe becomes less cruel and much more beautiful.
